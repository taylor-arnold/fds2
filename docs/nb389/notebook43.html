8. Compute the TF-IDF scores for `anno_en` with `min_df=0.01` and `max_df=0.5`.
Keep only the authors `"Jane Austen"`, `"A. A. Milne"`, and `"T. S. Eliot"` using

(

11. Build a 2D UMAP projection of the French pages, as in the chapter, with
